How the bid is run

Five kinds of work. One set of numbers.

The brief splits the settlement into mission systems, human factors, operations and infrastructure, structural engineering, and business and marketing. Kepler is organised the same way, because a department that cannot see its neighbours will invent a settlement the others cannot build.

Business and marketing holds the programme: the two-year road to Gusev, the upkeep the crew can actually perform, the unknowns, and the three balances where safety and the science spend the same mass. We do not keep a private version of the volume, the power, or the pack. A figure on this site and a different figure on an engineering slide is a defect, and it is closed before the proposal goes in.

The public line and the client line are the same sentence. Go to the ground, do the work, and leave it clean enough to go again. Marketing that promises a city, while the contract is for two containers, would fail the client in the room and the public outside it.
